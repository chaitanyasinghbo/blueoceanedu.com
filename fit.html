Right fit

This is selective by design.

The model works only when the family, student, counselor, and mentor all take the work seriously.

This program is for you if...

  • Your child is hardworking, ambitious, coachable, and willing to do the work.
  • You are focused on long-term development, not only the application.
  • You understand that elite outcomes require time, depth, and consistent execution.
  • You want the genuine attention of a founder-led team, not a junior counselor.

This program is not for you if...

  • You are looking for shortcuts or guaranteed admissions.
  • You want a done-for-you application service where the student does not grow.
  • You are looking for cheap counseling or a volume-based agency.
  • Your child is not serious about education, career, or future.

How to join

Admission is selective, and the process is short.

We take on a small number of students each year, because Dr. Sanjay works directly with every one of them. Selection is about fit, in both directions.

01

Diagnostic call

Parents, friends, and teachers have years of context on the student that we do not. This call closes that gap. We ask about the student and their current situation in depth, because two questions decide everything that follows. Would we be the best team for this student, and can the student handle our rigour.

02

Student essay

The student writes a short application essay. It shows us how the student thinks, how far the critical thinking goes, and who they are beyond what any call can surface. We read for seriousness and curiosity, never for polish.

03

Strategy call

If the student is selected, we come back with specifics. Given everything the diagnostic call surfaced, we walk you through exactly how we would help this student. If we are not the right fit, we say so plainly.

After a yes

The first month is mostly listening. We want to understand what the student has built, what they are curious about, and what they want the next years to look like. Then the plan gets specific. It names projects, deadlines, and the people the student will work with. We meet weekly from there.

What we never do

We do not promise admits, and any consultancy that does is not telling you the truth. What we promise is the work, the process, and the care. The results on our results page came from exactly that.

One fee covers everything described on this site. Mentorship, evaluation, projects, workshops, essays, applications, and every check-in between. It follows the April to March academic year and scales with the length of the program, so a student joining in grade 8 pays less each year than one joining in grade 12. We share the exact figures on the strategy call, once we know what the student actually needs.
