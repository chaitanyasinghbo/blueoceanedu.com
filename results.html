Student voices

In their own words.

Students who did the work, on what the years actually felt like.

Arunima

Admitted to Harvard
I was honestly very nervous about the whole process. Dr. Sanjay and the team were with me at every step, pushing me when I slacked and taking my calls at odd hours before deadlines. My essays went through more drafts than I can count, and every single one came back better. Thank you Team Blue Ocean for believing in me more than I believed in myself.

Anushka

Admitted to Columbia and Cambridge
When I joined, all I knew was that I wanted law. The team got me working at an actual criminal law firm, which gave me so much to write and talk about, and the mock interviews meant Cambridge did not scare me on the day. Getting Columbia and Cambridge both still feels unreal to me.

Manya

Admitted to Brown and Carnegie Mellon
I came in grade 10 with decent grades and no real direction. The team helped me find human-centric design and set up research and internships around it, and suddenly everything I did connected. Every essay draft came back within a day or two, with detailed feedback. I got Brown and Carnegie Mellon with a scholarship, and my parents still bring it up at every family function.

Tathagata

Admitted to Cornell and Johns Hopkins
Honestly, I walked in with just a list of rankings. The team kept asking what I actually enjoyed until we found fintech, and after that everything in my profile pointed one way. The counselors took my panic calls even at 2 am before the Cornell deadline. Forever grateful to Dr. Sanjay and the whole team.
