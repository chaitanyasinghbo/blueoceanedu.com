# blueoceanedu.com
#
# Everything on this site is meant to be read, by people and by machines.
# The AI crawlers are named explicitly rather than left to the wildcard,
# because a family asking ChatGPT or Gemini "who are the best admissions
# consultants in India" is the same family that would have typed it into
# Google, and a crawler that is not allowed cannot cite what it cannot read.
#
# Two kinds of bot are named below and they are not the same thing:
#
#   Search crawlers    build an index a person later searches.
#   Answer crawlers    fetch a page live to answer one question, right now.
#
# Blocking the second kind removes the site from the answer, not from the
# training set. Both are allowed.
#
# Nothing here is disallowed. Pages that should stay out of an index carry
# `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow">` instead, because a page
# blocked in robots.txt is never fetched, so the noindex is never read, and
# the URL can still surface as a bare link with no title under it. The pages
# that carry it are next-steps.html and the two generated landing folders.
